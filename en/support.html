Auris PEQ – Support

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Auris PEQ adds a parametric equalizer to every sound on your iPhone or iPad, by showing up as an audio output in Control Center. Requires iOS 27 or later.

Getting started

Open the app once, then open Control Center, long-press the audio card and choose “Auris PEQ” as the output. Everything you play now goes through the equalizer and comes out of the headphones or speaker you were using. Choose your previous output again in Control Center to stop.

I hear no sound

Open the app and check that it is running (it must stay available in the background), then pick Auris PEQ again in Control Center. If you used “Power off” in the app, reopen the app first.

The sound stutters for a few seconds

A short settling time right after choosing Auris PEQ is known; opening heavy apps such as Camera can also cause a brief glitch. If it persists, tell us your device, iOS version and output (see Contact).

Bypass

Use the Bypass switch in the app, or the Auris PEQ control in Control Center, to compare the sound with and without the equalizer.

iCloud sync

In Settings you can sync presets through your iCloud account. It is off by default and can be turned off at any time.

Tips

The app is free to use. Tips are optional and handled by the App Store; use Restore/Manage Subscriptions in your Apple Account for purchase questions.
