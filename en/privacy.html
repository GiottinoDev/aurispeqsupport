Auris PEQ – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Auris PEQ is a system-wide parametric equalizer for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what the app does with your data. Short version: it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Auris PEQ has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs. The developer runs no server and never receives any information about you or your device.

Audio

The sound you play is equalized on your device and sent to your headphones or speaker. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The microphone permission is requested only because iOS needs a play-and-record audio session to route the sound back to your output; the app does not listen to the microphone. The link between the system extension and the app runs entirely inside your device (127.0.0.1).

Settings and presets

Your equalizer settings and presets are stored on your device. You can optionally turn on iCloud sync in Settings (off by default): presets are then kept in Apple's iCloud key-value storage under your own Apple Account. The developer cannot read them; Apple's privacy policy applies to that storage. Turning the option off stops syncing.

Tips

The optional tips are In-App Purchases handled entirely by Apple. The developer receives no names, payment details or other personal information, only the aggregate sales reports Apple provides.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
